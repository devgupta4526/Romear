# Stage 00 assets

- `android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_romear.xml`: original geometric R lettermark
  authored for this repository; no third-party logo or asset source.
- Typography uses device/system sans and serif families on Android, system UI and
  Georgia fallbacks in web. No font files, licensing keys or remote font requests.
- `design_reference/`: user-supplied concept boards preserved as directions only.
  They are not bundled as screen backgrounds and none of their illustrative content
  is represented as real accounts, venues or activity.
- No photos, partner logos, analytics badges or fabricated social data are bundled.
